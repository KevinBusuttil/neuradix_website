About Neuradix

Software built around the system, not the fashion cycle.

Neuradix is the product technology brand of Busuttil Technologies Limited. Its products cover different domains, but share a preference for explicit architecture, local ownership, durable data and careful separation between implemented capability and product direction.

Why separate products?

ERP, manufacturing execution, retail, engineering design and robotics have different users, operational constraints and authority boundaries. Combining them into one monolith would make each product harder to adopt and harder to evolve. The Neuradix direction is therefore modular: each product should be useful on its own and integrate through explicit contracts and connectors when a workflow crosses domains.

  • Atlas — ERP and business management, including light manufacturing and a simpler integrated POS.
  • Manufacturing — dedicated manufacturing execution, material flow, quality and shop-floor traceability.
  • POS — dedicated advanced retail execution when tills, stores and retail controls need more depth than Atlas POS.
  • Origin — engineering definition, design and cross-discipline product intent.
  • Robotics Platform — machine control, automation, robotics and autonomous-system infrastructure.

Independently useful. Stronger together.

Neuradix Manufacturing should not require Atlas; Neuradix POS should not require Atlas; and Manufacturing should not require the Robotics Platform. Native Neuradix integrations can provide a deeper end-to-end workflow, while connectors allow organisations to retain existing ERP, engineering or automation systems.

Relationship with Busuttil Technologies

Busuttil Technologies Limited is the Malta-based company behind Neuradix. BTL remains the consulting and systems-engineering business; Neuradix is the product family. The two brands share engineering capability, but serve different commercial purposes.

How we communicate maturity

Neuradix products are at different stages. Public wording should distinguish what exists today from what is planned. Atlas is described in terms of commercialisation progress, Manufacturing and POS as product directions, Origin as architecture validation, and the Robotics Platform as a foundation prototype.
