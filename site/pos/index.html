Neuradix POS

Retail execution when the till becomes an operational system.

Neuradix POS is the planned dedicated point-of-sale product for businesses whose retail operation needs richer control, resilience and scale than the simpler POS included with Neuradix Atlas.

Product directionDedicated retail productERP-connectable
Neuradix POS is a separate advanced product direction. Atlas POS remains part of Atlas for straightforward small-business checkout and should not be presented as the same product with a different name.

A deliberate step up from Atlas POS.

Atlas POS is intended to cover everyday checkout as part of an ERP. Neuradix POS is intended for businesses where tills, cashiers, stores, hardware, availability and retail controls require their own operational product.

Advanced till operations

Richer cashier, shift, opening-float, suspended-sale, returns, tender and cash-up workflows with recovery designed for continuous retail use.

Multi-till and multi-store

Shared retail operations across multiple tills and locations, with centrally managed identity, permissions, stock visibility and operational state where the deployment requires it.

Retail rules

Product direction includes deeper promotions, discounts, loyalty, gift-value and customer-retail workflows beyond the intentionally smaller Atlas POS surface.

Hardware integration

A dedicated product can support receipt printers, scanners, cash drawers, customer displays, payment devices and other retail peripherals without pushing that complexity into Atlas.

Offline resilience

Checkout should continue through reasonable connectivity interruptions, with durable local transactions and controlled reconciliation when shared services become available again.

ERP connectors

Atlas can be the native Neuradix ERP integration, while the standalone POS should be able to connect to third-party ERP, inventory and commerce systems without requiring Atlas.

Atlas POS

Simple checkout, included with the ERP.

For smaller organisations with straightforward retail needs, Atlas can provide an integrated POS without requiring a second product.

Neuradix POS

A dedicated retail system when operations demand more.

Businesses can move to the standalone product when multi-till, multi-store, advanced controls, integrations or retail-specific reliability justify the additional operational depth.

POS is being defined as the advanced retail member of the Neuradix family.
