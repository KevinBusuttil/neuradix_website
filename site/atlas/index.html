Neuradix Atlas

Business operations that stay close to the business.

Atlas is being prepared as a local-first ERP and operations platform for smaller organisations: substantial accounting and stock capability without making permanent connectivity the centre of the product.

Commercialisation in progressSolo firstTeam follows
Atlas has substantial ERP functionality today, but commercial release is still gated by product hardening, migration, recovery, offline reliability, release engineering and supportability.

Solo when one operator needs control. Team when authority must be shared.

The product direction deliberately separates the two operating models rather than hiding them behind one vague cloud/local label.

Atlas Solo

Local-first and serverless for normal operation, with local accounting, stock, posting, numbering, backup and export responsibilities.

Atlas Team

A local-first client paired with backend authority for identity, membership, permissions, official posting, shared stock/accounting state, sync and audit.

Core ERP depth

Customers, suppliers, selling, buying, stock, payments, accounting foundations, tax/VAT and financial reporting are already substantial parts of the codebase.

Release discipline

The current commercialisation plan prioritises usability, migration, recovery and correctness ahead of adding more breadth.

Atlas is moving toward Solo commercial release.

Early commercial discussions can focus on owner-operated and small-business workflows where local-first operation matters.
