Neuradix Atlas

Business operations that stay close to the business.

Atlas is being prepared as a local-first ERP and business management platform for smaller organisations: substantial accounting, stock and operational capability without making permanent connectivity the centre of the product.

Atlas has substantial ERP functionality today, but commercial release is still gated by product hardening, migration, recovery, offline reliability, release engineering and supportability. Broad enough for everyday operations. Deliberately bounded where specialist execution begins.

Atlas keeps common ERP, light-manufacturing and straightforward checkout workflows together. Businesses that later need deeper factory-floor or retail execution can connect the dedicated Neuradix products instead of turning Atlas into a monolith.

Atlas Solo

Local-first and serverless for normal operation, with local accounting, stock, posting, numbering, backup and export responsibilities.

Atlas Team

A local-first client paired with backend authority for identity, membership, permissions, official posting, shared stock/accounting state, sync and audit.

Core ERP depth

Customers, suppliers, selling, buying, stock, payments, accounting foundations, tax/VAT and financial reporting are substantial parts of the product direction.

Light manufacturing

BOMs, work orders, basic material movement and production accounting can serve businesses that do not need a dedicated manufacturing execution layer.

Atlas POS

A simpler integrated checkout for small-business retail: everyday sales, standard payments, receipts and basic till workflows without the operational depth planned for Neuradix POS.

Specialist upgrade path

Neuradix Manufacturing and Neuradix POS are intended to integrate with Atlas when factory or retail execution becomes sophisticated enough to justify dedicated products.
